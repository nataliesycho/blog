I've kept notes for years, but almost always in private — half-finished
documents, a notes app full of fragments, the occasional voice memo I never
listen back to. This is my attempt to do it in public instead.

The premise is simple: writing forces the fuzzy thoughts to become concrete.
If I can't explain an idea plainly, I probably don't understand it yet.

## What you'll find here

Mostly essays and notes on things I'm working through:

- **Product** — how good software gets made, and why so little of it is.
- **Technology** — the tools I'm using and the ones I'm skeptical of.
- **Process** — how I think, decide, and change my mind.

None of it is meant to be the final word. Think of each post as a snapshot of
what I believed on the day I hit publish.

## A note on format

I'm keeping the design deliberately plain. No pop-ups, no newsletter gate on
the third paragraph, no autoplaying anything. Just text you can read.

> The best writing gets out of its own way.

If something here is useful to you, that's the whole point. Thanks for reading.
